About AmeriCal

One orchestra.
Shared responsibility.

A proposed guide for learning, rehearsing, and performing together.

Working draft · Not adopted regulations

Our shared commitments.

These suggestions are for discussion with orchestra leadership. Existing official policies remain the authority until a final version is approved.

01

Purpose & participation

Support the orchestra’s educational and community mission. Applications, placement, and performance participation should follow the orchestra’s published process.

02

Preparation & attendance

Practice assigned music, bring required materials, arrive ready to rehearse, and notify the orchestra promptly about absences or schedule conflicts.

03

Respect & cooperation

Treat fellow musicians, families, volunteers, and staff with courtesy. Listen attentively, care for shared equipment, and help maintain a focused rehearsal environment.

04

Safety & communication

Follow venue and supervision instructions. Parents and guardians should keep contact details current and communicate access needs or safety concerns directly to orchestra staff.

05

Performances & representation

Follow the confirmed call time, attire, and stage instructions for each performance. Obtain approval before speaking or making commitments on behalf of the orchestra.

06

Privacy & photographs

Handle personal information with care. Confirm the orchestra’s consent process before sharing identifiable photographs, recordings, or member details, especially those involving minors.

07

Stewardship & review

Use orchestra resources for approved purposes. Leadership should review this draft, clarify responsibilities, and publish any approved changes with an effective date.

Organizational structure was inspired by the ACCEF bylaws reference. These original draft guidelines do not adopt ACCEF’s governance, fees, tax status, or membership rules. AmeriCal’s formal governance and approval process still need confirmation.
