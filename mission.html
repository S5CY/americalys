About AmeriCal

Grow through music.
Give through music.

Our mission connects young musicians with one another and with the wider community.

Working draft · For orchestra review

Our mission.

To nurture young musicians through thoughtful orchestral training, meaningful performance, and a shared commitment to cultural exchange and community service.

01 / Learn

Develop the whole musician.

Build musical skill, attentive listening, confidence, and discipline through guided rehearsal and ensemble experience.

02 / Connect

Find a shared language.

Explore a rich range of musical traditions and bring generations and cultures together through performance.

03 / Give

Take music into the community.

Share live music in concert halls and community spaces, encouraging young people to serve with creativity and care.

04 / Belong

Grow together.

Foster a welcoming orchestra where musicians and families support each other’s learning and celebrate collective progress.

This proposed statement is awaiting AmeriCal review and approval. Its education, cultural exchange, and service themes were informed by ACCEF’s mission framework; it is not ACCEF’s statement or an adopted AmeriCal policy.
